# EchoRoute™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

**Short version: EchoRoute doesn't collect, send or share any of your data.**

## What the app does not do

- It has no internet permission, so it can't send anything off your phone.
- It doesn't use the microphone itself and doesn't ask for the `RECORD_AUDIO` permission. It never records or stores audio.
- It has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

## What is stored on your phone

Everything below stays in EchoRoute's private storage on your device:

- Your settings: the mode, the apps you picked and the effects chosen for each.
- The last state of the app (running, stopped, cleanup pending), so it can recover after a reboot or when Shizuku restarts.
- A small log file (`echoroute_app.log`) that helps you see what the app is doing. It can contain package names of apps that use the microphone, UIDs, session IDs and effect status.

You can clear the log from the Log tab. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

## Permissions

- Shizuku API permission: to apply the audio effects through Shizuku.
- Foreground service: to keep the controller running with a notification.
- Notifications: to show that notification.
- Receive boot completed: to restore the saved state after a reboot.

## Third parties

EchoRoute depends on Shizuku, which is a separate app with its own privacy practices.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
