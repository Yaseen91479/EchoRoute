2. The EchoRoute Idea

One of the most important distinctions is the difference between an idea and the expression of that idea.

An abstract idea, concept, procedure, system, process, or method of operation is generally not protected by copyright merely because it has been described or implemented.

The protection for EchoRoute therefore focuses on the concrete expression and implementation of the project.

This can include:

- the actual source code;
- the specific software implementation;
- original architecture documentation;
- original diagrams;
- technical specifications;
- written workflow descriptions;
- original terminology;
- UI implementation;
- original UI text;
- artwork and icons;
- other original project materials.

A person is not granted permission to copy, reproduce, distribute, modify, repackage, publish, or commercially exploit those protected materials merely because the repository is public.

At the same time, this guide does not claim an exclusive copyright over an abstract idea where applicable law does not provide such protection.

3. User Interface

The EchoRoute GUI should be treated separately from the rest of the project.

Protected rights may apply to original:

- UI source code;

However, generic controls, ordinary layouts, isolated colors, common design patterns, and purely functional elements are not automatically exclusive copyright property.

The project therefore claims rights in the original expression and implementation rather than in every individual visual building block.

7. Confidential Information and Trade Secrets

Material that has been publicly disclosed in the public GitHub repository should not be described as a trade secret merely by labeling it proprietary.

Trade-secret protection generally depends on information being kept confidential and on reasonable measures being taken to maintain secrecy.

Therefore, public EchoRoute source code and documentation should be treated primarily as copyrighted or otherwise protected public project materials rather than as confidential trade secrets.

Confidential development information that has not been publicly disclosed may be subject to different legal protections depending on the circumstances.

8. Patents

Copyright and patents are different rights.

Copyright generally protects expression, while patents may protect qualifying inventions subject to patent law, examination, eligibility, novelty, prior-art rules, disclosure requirements, and other legal conditions.

Nothing in the EchoRoute license grants patent rights.

If a specific technical invention may be patentable, it should be evaluated separately under the law applicable to the intended jurisdiction before making public disclosures that could affect patent rights.
